# DMS app: seller guide

Everything here is done from your phone, in the DMS app. Changes show on the website right away too.

## Getting into admin mode

1. Open the app → **Account** → **Log in** with your admin account.
2. The app switches to **admin mode** by itself. You'll see four tabs: **Dashboard · Products · Inbox · Settings**.
3. To see the shop the way shoppers do: **Settings** → **Switch to Shop**. To come back: **Account** → **Switch to Admin**.

Turn on **Settings → Notifications** so your phone pings you when a shopper sends a Direct Ask.

---

## Add a product from your phone

1. **Products** → **Add**.
2. Tap **Add photos from camera or gallery**.
   - **Take photo** uses the camera. **Choose from gallery** lets you pick up to 10 photos at once.
   - The app crops photos to the shop's 4:5 shape and makes them smaller, then uploads them. A pink bar shows the progress.
   - **Drag** a photo to change the order.
   - Tap the **★** to make a photo the main one (the first photo is always the main one).
   - Tap **×** to remove a photo.
3. Fill in:
   - **Name** (the web link is made from it automatically)
   - **Description**, **Category**
   - **Price** (optional):
     - leave it blank to show **"DM for Price 💌"**
     - **Sale price** shows the old price crossed out
     - turn **Show price to shoppers** off to keep a price private: only you see it, and shoppers see "DM for Price"
   - **Sizes** and **Colors**: tap a suggestion, or type one and press *done*. Tap a black chip to remove it.
   - **Material**
   - **Stock**: Available / Few Left / Sold Out, plus an optional quantity only you can see
   - **Visible in the shop**, **Featured** (Home carousel), **New**, **Best Seller**
4. Tap **Add product**. Wait for photos to finish uploading first.

### Edit, hide or delete

- Tap a product to edit it, then **Save changes**.
- **Swipe left** on a product in the list:
  - **Hide** takes it off the shop without deleting it. Swipe again to **Show** it.
  - **Delete** removes it and its photos for good. The app asks you first.
- **Bulk show/hide price** applies to every product in the current list. Pick a category first to change just that category.

### Categories

**Products → Categories**:

- **Add category**, or tap one to rename it, change its cover photo, or hide it
- the **arrows** change the order
- the **bin** deletes it (its products stay, uncategorized)

## Reply to chats (Inbox)

1. **Inbox** shows every Direct Ask, from the app and the website. Unread chats are at the top with a pink number.
   - Filters: **Open**, **Unread**, **Resolved**, **Archived**.
2. Tap a chat to open it.
   - Product cards show which piece the shopper asked about, with their size and color. Tap a card to open the product.
   - Type and tap the **↑** button to reply. Tap the **camera** to send a photo from the camera or gallery.
   - **Quick replies** appear above the keyboard. Tap one to insert it. Edit them in **Settings → Inbox quick replies**.
   - Under your last message, **Seen** means the shopper has read it.
3. Tap **⋯** (top right) to:
   - **Mark resolved**
   - **Archive** (hides the chat from Open)
   - see the shopper's **phone and address**

## Update social media links and shop info

**Settings**:

- **Shop:** name, tagline, logo
- **Home banner:** the big photo, headline and text at the top of Home
- **Social media:**
  - **Facebook** page link
  - **Messenger** username (the part after `m.me/`, e.g. `DMSPHIL`)
  - **Instagram** username (e.g. `dmsphil`)
  - **TikTok** link
  - other links (Shopee, Lazada…)

  Each has an on/off switch. If Messenger or Instagram is off or empty, that button disappears from product pages.
- **Contact:** phone, email, hours, location
- **Ordering, payment & shipping:** "How to order" text, payment notes, shipping notes, couriers (J&T Express, LBC)

Tap **Save settings** at the bottom when you're done.

## Dashboard

- How many products you have (and how many are hidden)
- Unread messages and open chats
- **Inquiry taps** in the last 30 days: Messenger vs Instagram vs Direct Ask
- **Most viewed** products, and products per category
- **Recent inquiries**

Pull down on any screen to refresh.
