# DMS Seller Guide 💌

Everything you need to run your shop. No coding needed.

**To start:** log in at **yourshop.com/login** with your admin account, then tap the **dashboard icon** in the top bar, or go to **yourshop.com/admin**.

On your phone, the admin menu is at the bottom of the screen: **Dashboard · Products · Categories · Inbox · Drop-offs · Settings**.

---

## Add a product

1. Go to **Products → Add product**.
2. Type the **Product name**. The web address (slug) fills itself in.
3. Write a **Description**: fit, feel, lining, closures. Shoppers love details!
4. **Photos:** drag photos into the box, or tap **browse** to pick them from your phone.
   - The **first photo is the main photo**. It's shown on cards and in Messenger previews.
   - Tap ⭐ on any photo to make it the main one, use the arrows to reorder, and the 🗑 to remove.
   - Photos are shrunk and compressed for you automatically. Portrait shots (4:5) look best.
5. **Sizes and colors:** tap the suggestions (XS, S, M… / Cream, Blush…) or type your own and press **Enter**.
6. **Material:** for example "Faux fur, satin lining".
7. **Price:**
   - To **show the price**, keep **Show price on site** on and type the amount. Add a **Sale price** if you like, and the old price shows crossed out.
   - For **DM for Price 💌**, switch **Show price on site** off, or leave the price blank. You can still type your price there for your own reference. Shoppers will never see it.
8. **Organize:** pick a **Category**, a **Stock status** (Available, Few Left, Sold Out), and a quantity.
9. **Badges and visibility:**
   - **Featured** puts it in the home page "New Arrivals" carousel.
   - **New** and **Best Seller** show as little tags on the photo.
   - **Visible on site** off hides it from shoppers. Good for drafts!
10. Tap **Add product**. Done! ✨

### Other product tricks

- **Edit:** tap any product in the list.
- **Duplicate** (the copy icon): great for the same jacket in a new color. The copy starts hidden, so you can edit it before publishing.
- **Delete** (the trash icon): asks you to confirm first.
- **Show or hide several prices at once:** tick the boxes beside products, then tap **Show price** or **Hide price** in the bar that appears.
- **The eye icon** quickly shows or hides a product.

## Manage categories

Go to **Categories**.

- **New category:** give it a name, an optional description, and a **cover image** (shown on the home page tiles).
- **Rename or change the cover:** tap the ✏️ pencil.
- **Reorder:** use the ↑ ↓ arrows. **The top category is shown biggest on the home page**, so keep *Cropped Fur Jackets* first.
- **Hide:** tap the 👁 eye. Hidden categories and their products disappear from the shop.
- **Delete:** tap the 🗑. Products inside are **not** deleted. They just become "Uncategorized".

You can sell anything. Just add a category for it!

## Manage drop-off points and partner shops

Go to **Drop-offs**. These are the pins on the map on your home page and on the **Drop-offs** page.

- **Add drop-off point:** fill in the details, then **tap the map** where it is, or drag the pin.
  - **Name:** e.g. "Session Road Drop Point" or your partner's shop name
  - **Type:** **Drop-off / meet-up point** (rose pin) or **Partner shop** (black pin)
  - **Area / town:** Baguio City, La Trinidad, Tuba, Itogon… Shoppers can filter the map by town.
  - **Landmark, schedule, notes:** e.g. "Near the Municipal Hall", "Mon–Sat, 10AM–6PM", "Message us first"
- **Edit** with the ✏️ pencil, **hide** with the 👁 eye, **reorder** with ↑ ↓, and **delete** with the 🗑.
- Shoppers get a **Directions** button on each point that opens Google Maps.

The sample points are placeholders in real Baguio and La Trinidad locations. Replace them with your real spots and partners before you launch!

**Couriers:** J&T Express and LBC are listed under **Settings → Ordering, payment & shipping → Couriers**. Add or remove couriers there.

## Update your social media and contact links

Go to **Settings**.

### Messenger, Instagram, Facebook, TikTok

Each has an **on/off switch**. When a link is switched off or left empty, it disappears from the site, and its **Message** button on product pages hides too.

| Field | What to type | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Facebook Page URL | Your page's full link | `https://facebook.com/dmsboutique` |
| Messenger username | The part after `m.me/` | `dmsboutique` |
| Instagram username | Your handle (with or without @) | `dms.boutique` |
| TikTok URL | Your full TikTok link | `https://tiktok.com/@dms.boutique` |

**Finding your Messenger username:** open your Facebook Page and look at its web address. In `facebook.com/dmsboutique`, the username is `dmsboutique`. To test it, open `https://m.me/dmsboutique` in your browser.

**Other links** (Shopee, Lazada, Linktree…): tap **Add link**, then type a label and the link.

### Also in Settings

- **Shop name, tagline, logo, and hero banner** (the big photo and headline at the top of the home page)
- **Contact info:** phone or Viber, email, business hours, and location (shown in the footer)
- **How to Order, Payment, and Shipping notes:** shown on the home page and every product page
- **Currency:** ₱ by default
- **Inbox quick replies:** your saved answers, like "Yes, available! 💕"

Tap **Save settings**. Changes are live on the site right away.

## Answer Direct Ask messages

Go to **Inbox**. Unread chats are listed first, and the Inbox tab shows a dot when something is new.

- When a shopper asks about a product, a **product card** (photo, name, price, size and color) appears at the top of their message.
- Tap ⚡ to insert a **quick reply**, or 📷 to send a photo.
- When you're done, tap **Resolve**. If the shopper writes again, the chat reopens by itself. Use **Archive** to tidy away old chats.
- Shoppers' phone numbers and addresses, if they added them, appear under their name.

**Get notified on your phone:** on the Dashboard, tap **Notify me of replies** and allow notifications. On iPhone, first install the app (see below).

---

## Install the app on your phone

- **Android:** open your site in Chrome and tap **Install DMS App** (in the banner or the footer).
- **iPhone:** open your site in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

---

## Your dashboard numbers

- **New inquiries / unread:** Direct Ask chats waiting for you
- **DM button clicks:** how many times shoppers tapped Messenger, Instagram, or Direct Ask (all-time and this week)
- **Most viewed:** your most-looked-at products
- **Products per category:** a quick stock overview

Happy selling! 💕
